# Project Overview
**Goal:** a polished, responsive DTC-style skincare landing page without frameworks.
**Audience:** young adults and skincare enthusiasts.
**Design:** editorial serif headlines, arched hero image, restrained palette, one deep-green ingredient section for contrast, subtle motion only (hero entrance, reveals, hover, accordion).
**Content policy:** no clinical, SPF, certification or medical claims; testimonials and products are fictional and labeled as samples/demo.
**Structure:** announcement > nav > hero > story > shop > values > ingredients > ritual > testimonials > newsletter > FAQ > footer.
**State:** bag items stored in `localStorage` key `glownest_bag_v1`.
**External dependencies:** Google Fonts and Unsplash images only.
